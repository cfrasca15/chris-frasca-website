My story

From Ventura to Orange County

I grew up in Ventura and lived there until I was 19. About twelve years ago I moved to Orange County after meeting my wife, Lauren, who is from San Clemente. We've been here ever since.

I started in Medicare in August 2016. I had just graduated from college and was looking for a career. A close friend of mine, who had worked at a Medicare health plan for a few years, had just become a broker, and that's how I first learned about this work.

I soon realized how much help seniors needed, and I liked the idea of working for myself. That's still true today. I get to sit down with people, hear what matters to them, and help them go in the right direction.

How I work

I listen first

Before I talk about any plan, I ask about your doctors, your medications, and what you want from your coverage. Then I put more than ten years of experience to work, so you can go in the right direction without the confusion.

My help costs you nothing. Insurance carriers pay licensed agents, not you, and your premium is the same whether you enroll through me or directly with Medicare. I stay in touch after you enroll, with a review each year to make sure your plan still fits.

I represent a limited number of insurance organizations, so I can't offer every plan available in your area. You can always get information on all of your options from Medicare.gov or 1-800-MEDICARE.
