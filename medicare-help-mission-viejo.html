Medicare questions from Mission Viejo residents

Can we meet in person in Mission Viejo?

Yes. I can meet locally, talk by phone, or connect by video, whichever is easiest for you. You can also come to one of my no-cost workshops held at the Norman P. Murray Community and Senior Center.

What if my doctor or hospital changes which plans it works with?

It happens: networks between insurers and medical groups change from year to year. That is why I check your specific doctors against each plan I show you, and why a yearly review is part of the service. Depending on the situation, you may be able to change plans outside the usual enrollment windows.

Do you hold Medicare events in Mission Viejo?

Yes. See the events page for upcoming no-cost workshops. They are educational and there is no obligation.

What does it cost to work with you?

Nothing. Licensed insurance agents are paid a commission by the insurance carrier, not by you. Your premium is the same whether you enroll through an agent or directly with Medicare, and there is no fee for a consultation, plan review, or yearly review.
