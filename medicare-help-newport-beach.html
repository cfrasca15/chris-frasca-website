Medicare questions from Newport Beach residents

I see doctors at Hoag. How do I make sure I can keep them?

Network participation is set by each plan and can change every year, so there is no one answer for every plan. I look up your doctors and the hospital you prefer under each plan I show you. Some options, such as Original Medicare with a Medigap plan, do not use a network at all and work with providers that accept Medicare.

What if my plan's network is changing next year?

Your plan has to notify you in writing each fall. If it affects your doctors, the Annual Enrollment Period (October 15 to December 7) is the time to review other plans, with changes taking effect January 1.

Is a PPO or an HMO better if I want more choice of doctors?

PPO plans generally allow more flexibility to see providers outside a network, often at a higher cost, and HMOs usually cost less but keep you within a network. Which fits depends on your doctors and your budget, and I can show you both.

What does it cost to work with you?

Nothing. Licensed insurance agents are paid a commission by the insurance carrier, not by you. Your premium is the same whether you enroll through an agent or directly with Medicare, and there is no fee for a consultation, plan review, or yearly review.
