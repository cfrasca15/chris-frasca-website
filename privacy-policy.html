Privacy Policy & Messaging Terms

What information I collect, how I use it, and the choices you have.

Last updated: October 3, 2026

This policy applies to chrisfrascainsurance.com, operated by Chris Frasca Insurance Services (“I,” “me,” “my”), a licensed California insurance agency. This website is not connected with or endorsed by the United States government or the federal Medicare program.

Information I collect

  • Information you give me: your name, email address, phone number, and the message or notes you submit through the contact form, the event RSVP form, or when you book a consultation.
  • Information collected automatically: basic technical information such as your browser type and pages visited. If you arrive from an online ad, advertising measurement tools, such as Google Ads conversion tracking, may use cookies to measure which ads led to a contact. These cookies are only set if you click Accept in the cookie notice.

Please don’t put sensitive details, such as your Social Security number, Medicare number, or health information, in the website forms. I will ask for anything I need during a conversation.

How I use it

  • To respond to your request and schedule or confirm appointments and events.
  • To contact you, with your permission, about Medicare plans and options.
  • To improve the website and measure whether my advertising is working.

Who I share it with

I do not sell your personal information. I do not share your phone number or your text-message opt-in information with third parties for their own marketing.

I use service providers who process information on my behalf to run this website, such as website hosting (Netlify), email delivery (Resend), event registration records (Airtable), and appointment scheduling (Calendly). If you choose to enroll in a plan, I share the information needed to complete the enrollment with the insurance carrier and its enrollment systems. I may also disclose information when required by law.

Calls, texts, and email

When you check the permission box on a form, you agree that I may contact you by phone call, text message, and email about Medicare plans and your request. Consent is not a condition of receiving any services or of buying any insurance product.

  • Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply.
  • To stop text messages, reply STOP. For help, reply HELP or call 949-259-6744.
  • To stop calls or emails, tell me on a call, reply to any email, or contact me using the details below.

Calls with me about Medicare plans may be recorded, as required by federal Medicare rules. Recordings are kept for the period those rules require.

How long I keep information

I keep your information as long as needed to respond to you, serve you as a client, and meet legal and regulatory requirements. Insurance and Medicare rules may require me to keep some records for several years.

Your choices and rights

You can ask me what information I have about you, ask me to correct it, or ask me to delete it, subject to legal record-keeping requirements. California residents have additional rights under California privacy law, including the right to know, delete, and correct personal information, and the right to opt out of its sale or sharing. I do not sell personal information.

Security

I take reasonable steps to protect your information, including encrypted connections to this website. No method of transmission over the internet is completely secure.

Changes to this policy

If I change this policy, I will post the updated version here and update the date at the top.
