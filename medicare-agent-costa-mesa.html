Medicare questions from Costa Mesa residents

Which Medicare options give me the widest choice of doctors?

Original Medicare combined with a Medigap (Supplement) plan generally lets you see any provider that accepts Medicare, with no network to worry about. The trade-off is a monthly premium. Medicare Advantage plans often cost less per month but use a network. I walk through both so you can compare.

My plan is changing and I got a letter. What now?

That is probably an Annual Notice of Change. Carriers send them every fall. Before the December 7 deadline, it is worth comparing your current plan against other options, and I can do that with you at no cost.

Can I do this by phone or video?

Yes. Phone, video, and in-person meetings are all available, and family members are welcome to join.

What does it cost to work with you?

Nothing. Licensed insurance agents are paid a commission by the insurance carrier, not by you. Your premium is the same whether you enroll through an agent or directly with Medicare, and there is no fee for a consultation, plan comparison, or yearly review.
