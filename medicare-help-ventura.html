Can you help me if you are not in my city?

Yes. Most of my Ventura County clients work with me by phone or video, from enrollment through the yearly review. I'm glad to meet in person when I'm in the area.

Why does my county matter?

Medicare Advantage and Part D plans are offered by county, and the plans, premiums and networks can differ from one county to the next. I check the plans for your exact address.

Many California plans are built around medical groups. How do you handle that?

In California, many HMO-style plans coordinate care through a medical group. I check that your doctors and their group work with each plan I show you, before you enroll.

What does it cost to work with you?

Nothing. Licensed insurance agents are paid a commission by the insurance carrier, not by you. Your premium is the same whether you enroll through an agent or directly with Medicare, and there is no fee for a consultation, plan review, or yearly review.
