About Chris

Your independent guide, not a call-center script.

I'm an independent insurance agent, which means I'm not limited to selling one company's plans. My job is to take the burden of figuring out Medicare away from you — whether you're aging into Medicare at 65, retiring off your or your spouse's group health plan, or simply need help finding the right plan for your needs.

All of my services are completely free, and my goal is to find the right option for you and your needs, year after year — not just during your first enrollment.

Outside of work, you'll usually find me outdoors — soccer, golf, backpacking — or at home with my wife Lauren and our two cats.

What I compare for you

  • Original Medicare vs. Medicare Advantage
  • Medigap (Supplement) plans
  • Part D prescription drug coverage
  • Your specific doctors' network status
  • Your specific medications' cost under each plan
  • Dental, vision, and hearing add-ons

What it costs you

Nothing. I'm paid by the insurance carrier, the same way every licensed agent is — your premium is identical whether you enroll through me, another agent, or on your own.
