Common questions

Medicare questions from Laguna Hills residents

I live in a senior community. Does that change my Medicare options?

Generally no. Plan availability depends on where you live (your county and zip code), not on the type of community. What matters is your doctors, prescriptions, and budget.

Can you meet with my spouse and me together?

Absolutely. Each of you has your own plan decisions, but it is often easier to talk them through together, and I am happy to include a son or daughter by phone or video.

I got a letter saying my plan is changing. What should I do?

That is likely an Annual Notice of Change. Carriers send them every fall. It is worth reviewing your current plan against other options before the December 7 deadline, and I can do that with you at no cost.

What does it cost to work with you?

Nothing. Licensed insurance agents are paid a commission by the insurance carrier, not by you. Your premium is the same whether you enroll through an agent or directly with Medicare, and there is no fee for a consultation, plan review, or yearly review.
