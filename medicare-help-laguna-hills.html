Common questions

Medicare questions from Laguna Hills residents

I live in a senior community. Does that change my Medicare options?

No. Your plan options depend on where you live, meaning your county and zip code, not on the type of community. What matters most is your doctors, your prescriptions, and your budget.

Can you meet with my spouse and me together?

Of course. Each of you has your own plan decisions, but it's often easier to talk them through together. If a son or daughter wants to join by phone or video, that's fine too.

I got a letter saying my plan is changing. What should I do?

That's likely your Annual Notice of Change. Plans send them every fall. Look it over before the December 7 deadline, and I'm happy to go through it with you and review your other options at no cost.

What does it cost to work with you?

Nothing. The insurance company pays me a commission, not you, so your premium is the same whether you enroll through me or directly with Medicare. There's no fee for a consultation, a plan review, or the yearly check-in.
