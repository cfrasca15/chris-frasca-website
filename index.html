Why work with an independent licensed insurance agent

I work for you — not an insurance company.

My goal is to take the burden of figuring out Medicare off your shoulders. Whether you're aging into Medicare at 65, retiring off your group health plan (or your spouse's), or simply want a second opinion on your current plan, I'll make it as simple as possible.

When I'm not helping folks with Medicare, I'm usually outside — soccer, golf, backpacking — or at home with my wife Lauren and our two cats. I like to think that same straightforwardness carries over into how I explain your options.

Turning 65?

Enrollment windows and penalties are easy to get wrong. I'll walk you through exactly what to do and when.

Leaving a group plan?

Retiring or losing employer coverage has its own rules and deadlines — I'll help you avoid a coverage gap.

Reviewing your current plan?

Plans change every year. I'll check yours annually, at no cost, to make sure it's still the right fit.

Helping a parent or loved one?

You're welcome to join the call by phone or video. I'll explain things plainly so everyone in the family is on the same page.

What to expect

Your first conversation

It starts with a no-cost call, video chat, or in-person meeting — whichever you prefer. I'll ask about your doctors, your prescriptions, and what you want to spend, then review the plan options available where you live.

There's no pressure to decide on the spot. Take the review home, talk it over with family, and enroll when you're ready — with me, or not at all.
