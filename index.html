Why work with an independent agent

I work for you — not an insurance company.

My goal is to take the burden of figuring out Medicare off your shoulders. Whether you're aging into Medicare at 65, retiring off your or your spouse's group health plan, or simply want a second opinion on your current plan, I'll make it as simple as possible.
