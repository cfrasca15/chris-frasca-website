Common questions

Medicare questions from San Juan Capistrano residents

My doctors are in different cities. Does that matter?

It can. Each plan decides which doctors, hospitals, and pharmacies are in its network, and that can change from year to year. I look up each of yours under every plan I show you.

What if I want a plan with more flexibility?

Some options, such as Original Medicare with a Medigap plan, do not use a network and work with providers that accept Medicare. Others, such as many Medicare Advantage plans, use networks. Which fits depends on your doctors and your budget, and I can show you both.

Can we meet in person?

Yes. I can meet in person in Orange County, talk by phone, or connect by video, and you are welcome to bring a family member.

What does it cost to work with you?

Nothing. Licensed insurance agents are paid a commission by the insurance carrier, not by you. Your premium is the same whether you enroll through an agent or directly with Medicare, and there is no fee for a consultation, plan review, or yearly review.
