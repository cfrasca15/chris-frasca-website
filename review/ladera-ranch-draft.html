Turning 65, still working, or helping a parent?

Ladera Ranch is an unincorporated community in south Orange County, next to Mission Viejo and San Juan Capistrano. The Medicare questions I hear from families here usually fall into three groups:

  • Turning 65. Your Initial Enrollment Period is a 7-month window around your 65th birthday month. Read the turning-65 guide →
  • Still working. If you have group health coverage through your own or your spouse’s current job, you may be able to wait on Part B without a late penalty. Check with your benefits office first. Medicare.gov: Working past 65

Common questions

Medicare questions from Ladera Ranch residents

When can I sign up for Medicare?

Your Initial Enrollment Period is a 7-month window. It starts 3 months before the month you turn 65, includes your birthday month, and ends 3 months after it.

I'm still working at 65. Do I need Part B?

It depends on your coverage. If you have group health coverage through your own or your spouse's current job, you may be able to wait until that job ends to sign up for Part B without a late penalty. Check with your benefits office, and I can walk through it with you.

Can I help my parent with their Medicare plan?

Yes. With your parent's permission, you're welcome to join in person, by phone, or by video. I'll review their doctors, medications, and budget with both of you.

What does it cost to work with you?

Nothing. The insurance company pays me a commission, not you, so your premium is the same whether you enroll through me or directly with Medicare. There's no fee for a consultation, a plan review, or the yearly check-in.
