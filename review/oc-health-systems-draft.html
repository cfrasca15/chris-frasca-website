The short answer

A hospital taking your plan is not the same as your doctor being in it

  • Original Medicare with a Medigap plan lets you see any provider that accepts Medicare. No network, no referrals.
  • Medicare Advantage has a network, and in California that usually comes down to your doctor’s medical group as well as the hospital.
  • Networks change every year, so it pays to check each fall, between October 15 and December 7.
The main systems

The health systems most Orange County members ask about

This is a plain-English look at how each system is set up and what has changed for 2027. It is not a list of every plan, and each system keeps its own current list on its website.

Hoag

Hoag has two hospitals, Hoag Hospital Newport Beach and Hoag Hospital Irvine, and two medical groups, Hoag Medical Group and Hoag Physician Partners.

Hoag’s plan list is for Hoag Medical Group, so a plan that works for your primary care doctor may not include every specialist. The big change for 2027 is SCAN. Hoag’s contract with SCAN ends December 31, so SCAN HMO members will find Hoag out of network starting January 1. I explain that on my Hoag and SCAN page.

Where to check: Hoag, Insurance Accepted and Hoag, Medicare.

Providence

Providence runs St. Joseph Hospital in Orange, St. Jude Medical Center, and Mission Hospital in Mission Viejo and Laguna Beach. The Heritage medical groups, St. Joseph, St. Jude and Mission, sit behind them. To use a Providence hospital, your doctor’s group and your plan both need a contract with it.

Two things changed. UnitedHealthcare Medicare Advantage plans stopped working with Providence’s California network on January 1, 2026. And Providence Health Plan, the insurance company, is leaving Medicare Advantage after this year. That is the insurance plan, not the hospitals, which may still be available under other plans. If you have a Providence Sycamore plan, see my Providence Sycamore page.

Where to check: Providence Southern California, Insurance Accepted, the UnitedHealthcare notice and Providence Health Plan’s update.

UCI Health

UCI Health’s main Orange County locations are UCI Medical Center in Orange and UCI Health Irvine, and its doctors belong to UCI University Physicians & Surgeons.

Of the four, this is the easiest to check, because its Medicare page lists the Medicare Advantage plans it accepts starting January 1, 2027. The list can still change, so I confirm it with the carrier before you enroll.

Where to check: UCI Health, Medicare insurance information.

MemorialCare

MemorialCare’s Orange County hospitals are Orange Coast Medical Center in Fountain Valley and Saddleback Medical Center in Laguna Hills. Its doctors belong to MemorialCare Medical Group, MemorialCare Independent Physicians or Edinger Medical Group.

This is the system where the medical group matters most. MemorialCare lists its plans group by group, so the same carrier can be accepted by one group and not by another. For 2027 it marks some HMO plans, including Aetna and Blue Shield, as no longer accepted, depending on the group. Its hospital pages still use older plan names, so go by your group’s list.

Where to check: MemorialCare, Health Insurance Plans.

What I check

Why I look at your medical group, not just your hospital

Many California Medicare Advantage HMOs contract with medical groups, and your primary care doctor belongs to one. A hospital can take a plan while your doctor’s group does not, or the other way around. So when I review your options, I check your doctor by name, their medical group, and the hospital you would use, against each plan.

If your plan changes

If your plan no longer includes your doctor or hospital

  1. Read your Annual Notice of Change or the notice your plan sent. It says what is changing.
  2. Write down your doctors, your hospital, your prescriptions and doses, and your pharmacy.
  3. Check each one against the plans you are considering, using the doctor’s name and medical group.
  4. Decide before December 7 if you want the change to start January 1. If you miss it, you may qualify for a special enrollment period, depending on your situation.
  5. Keep your current plan until the new coverage and its start date are confirmed.

If your plan is ending altogether, see my pages for Aetna PPO members and Providence Sycamore members.

Original Medicare and Medigap

If you would rather not deal with a network

Original Medicare lets you see any provider that accepts Medicare, at any of these systems. A Medigap (Supplement) plan helps with out-of-pocket costs, and a separate Part D plan covers prescriptions. Medigap premiums are usually higher than a Medicare Advantage plan, and it may ask health questions, though you may qualify without them in some situations. I will check which applies to you.

Common questions

Questions about Orange County health systems and Medicare

What is a medical group?

It is the organization your primary care doctor belongs to. Many California Medicare Advantage HMOs contract with medical groups, so a plan can include a hospital without including your doctor’s group. That is why I check your doctor by name and medical group, not only the hospital.

Can I keep my doctor if my plan is ending?

It depends on the plan you move to. Some Medicare Advantage plans may include your doctor and some may not. Original Medicare with a Medigap plan lets you see any provider that accepts Medicare. I check each of your doctors against your options before you decide.

Will I still be able to see Providence doctors?

Possibly. Providence Health Plan is leaving Medicare Advantage, but that is the insurance company, not the hospitals and doctors, which may still be available under other plans. I check your doctors against each plan before you enroll.

Do I need a referral to see a specialist?

Many Medicare Advantage HMO plans ask you to use a medical group and may require a referral for a specialist. Original Medicare does not. I explain how each option works for you.

Which health system is best for Medicare?

I do not rank them. The right fit depends on where you live, which doctors you see, your prescriptions and what you can afford. I show you the options and the costs, and you decide.
