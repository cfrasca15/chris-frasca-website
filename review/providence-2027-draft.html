What is changing

Providence is leaving Medicare Advantage

  • Providence is leaving Medicare Advantage. Its plans will not be available in 2027 or beyond.
  • If you are in a 2026 Providence Medicare Advantage plan, coverage ends December 31, 2026, and your current coverage continues until then.
  • Orange County is where Providence Health Assurance offers its California Medicare Advantage plan, Providence Medicare Sycamore + Rx (HMO). It is a Medicare Advantage Prescription Drug (MAPD) plan, and that plan is ending.
  • The plan is ending, not Providence the medical system. Providence hospitals and doctors will still accept certain Medicare Advantage plans in 2027. Which ones, and whether your doctors are in network, depends on the plan you choose.

Sources: Providence Health Plan, Medicare Advantage Plans and its coverage update. Check the notice Providence sent you for the details that apply to you.

Your options before December 7

Three ways to handle it

1. Review another Medicare Advantage plan

You can join a different Medicare Advantage plan available in Orange County. Plans differ in premium, copays, drug coverage and, in California, the medical groups they use. I check each of your doctors and prescriptions before you enroll.

2. Original Medicare with Medigap and Part D

Original Medicare lets you see any provider that accepts Medicare. A Medigap (Supplement) plan helps with out-of-pocket costs, and a separate Part D plan covers prescriptions. Medigap may ask health questions, though some people qualify without them. I will check which applies to you.

3. Not choosing a plan

If your plan ends and you do not choose a new one, you will have Original Medicare only starting January 1. Original Medicare does not include prescription drug coverage, so you would have no drug coverage unless you add a Part D plan. It is worth a conversation before the year ends.

My job is to show you the options and the costs, not to tell you which one to pick. There is no cost to you for a review.

If you are thinking about Medigap

Could you get Medigap without health questions?

Outside your first Medigap enrollment window, a Medigap insurer can usually ask about your health, and it can charge you more or turn you down. The exceptions are called guaranteed issue rights. When you have one, the insurer has to sell you certain Medigap plans regardless of your health.

When your Medicare Advantage plan is ending, you may qualify for guaranteed issue into a Medigap plan. Whether it applies to you depends on your situation, and I check that with you before you leave your current plan.

  • Keep every letter you get from Providence about this change. If you have a guaranteed issue right, the letter can help show it.
  • These rights have deadlines. It is better to ask now than after January 1.
  • Don’t cancel your current plan until your new coverage and its start date are confirmed.

Source: Medicare.gov, When can I buy a Medigap policy? For California rules, California’s HICAP program offers no-cost Medicare counseling.

Key dates

Why December 7 matters

  • October 15 – December 7: Annual Enrollment Period. You can change Medicare Advantage or Part D plans, and the change starts January 1.
  • After December 7: You may qualify for a special enrollment period when your plan is ending. I can tell you how it applies to you.
Common questions

Questions from Providence members

Is my Providence Medicare plan really ending?

Yes. The Providence Medicare Sycamore + Rx (HMO) MAPD plan is ending. Providence is leaving Medicare Advantage, so its plans will not be available in 2027 or beyond, and coverage ends December 31, 2026. Your Providence notice has the details. If you did not get one or you are not sure, call me and we can go through your coverage together.

What happens if I do nothing?

If your plan ends and you do not choose a new one, you will have Original Medicare only starting January 1. Original Medicare does not include prescription drug coverage, so you would need to add a Part D plan to have drug coverage. I can walk you through what that would look like for you.

Do I have to decide by December 7?

Annual Enrollment runs October 15 to December 7, and a change you make in that period starts January 1. You may also qualify for a special enrollment period when your plan is ending. I can go over how it applies to you.

What about my prescriptions?

Original Medicare does not include Part D, so you would need a Medicare plan with drug coverage or a separate Part D plan. I check your prescriptions, doses and pharmacy against each option I review.

Will my doctors and hospital still be covered?

It depends on the plan you choose. Providence as a medical system will still accept certain Medicare Advantage plans in 2027, so your Providence doctors and hospital may still be available, depending on the plan you choose. I check each of your doctors before you enroll.

Can I buy Medigap without health questions?

Possibly. Because your plan is ending, you may qualify for guaranteed issue into a Medigap plan, which means no health questions. Whether it applies to you depends on your situation, so I check it with you before you change anything.

Will switching plans cost me more?

It depends on the plan. Medicare Advantage plans are still available, and some may be similar to your current plan. Premiums, copays, and your prescriptions all differ. Medigap premiums are usually higher than Medicare Advantage plans. I walk through your doctors, your drugs, and the total cost under each option I review so you can decide.

Is this the same as the Hoag and SCAN change?

No. That is a different change involving a different plan and provider. If you got a letter from Hoag, see my Hoag and SCAN page.
