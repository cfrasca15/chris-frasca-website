What Aetna has said

If your Aetna plan is being discontinued

  • Aetna says it discontinues some plans each year, and that a discontinued plan’s current benefits are active through December 31, 2026.
  • Aetna says that as of January 1, 2027, a member without a new plan is covered only by Original Medicare, which does not include the extra benefits a Medicare Advantage plan has.
  • Aetna’s Medicare Advantage PPO plans in Orange County and Ventura County are among the plans being discontinued for 2027. Check the notice Aetna sent you to see how it applies to your plan.
  • Orange County: Aetna Medicare Signature (PPO), Aetna Medicare Enhanced (PPO) and Aetna Medicare Eagle (PPO).
  • Ventura County: Aetna Medicare Enhanced (PPO) and Aetna Medicare Eagle (PPO).
  • Aetna also lists a special enrollment period for members of a discontinued plan, December 8, 2026 to February 28, 2027.

Sources: Aetna, Ways to enroll and its 2027 Medicare guidebook. Your plan name is on your member ID card and your notice. Plan names and service areas can change.

Your options before December 7

Three ways to handle it

1. Review another Medicare Advantage plan

You can join a different Medicare Advantage plan available where you live. Some work like a PPO and some are HMOs, which usually ask you to use a medical group and may need a referral for specialists. Plans also differ in premium, copays and drug coverage. I check each of your doctors and prescriptions before you enroll.

2. Original Medicare with Medigap and Part D

Original Medicare lets you see any provider that accepts Medicare, with no network and no referrals. A Medigap (Supplement) plan helps with out-of-pocket costs, and a separate Part D plan covers prescriptions. Medigap may ask health questions, though some people qualify without them. I will check which applies to you.

3. Not choosing a plan

If your Medicare Advantage plan ends and you do not choose a new one, Aetna and Medicare.gov both say you are covered only by Original Medicare starting January 1. That does not include most prescription drug coverage, so it is worth a conversation before the year ends.

My job is to show you the options and the costs, not to tell you which one to pick. There is no cost to you for a review.

If you are thinking about Medigap

Could you get Medigap without health questions?

Outside your first Medigap enrollment window, a Medigap insurer can usually ask about your health, and it can charge you more or turn you down. The exceptions are called guaranteed issue rights. When you have one, the insurer has to sell you certain Medigap plans regardless of your health.

Medicare.gov lists a guaranteed issue right for people whose Medicare Advantage plan leaves Medicare or stops serving their area. The window to apply is limited, so it is better to ask now than after your coverage ends. Whether it applies to you depends on your situation, and I check that with you before you leave your current plan.

  • Keep every letter you get from Aetna about this change. If you have a guaranteed issue right, the letter can help show it.
  • These rights have deadlines. It is better to ask now than after January 1.
  • Don’t cancel your current plan until your new coverage and its start date are confirmed.

Source: Medicare.gov, When can I buy a Medigap policy? For California rules, California’s HICAP program offers no-cost Medicare counseling.

Key dates

Why December 7 matters

  • October 15 – December 7: Annual Enrollment Period. You can change Medicare Advantage or Part D plans, and the change starts January 1.
  • After December 7: Aetna lists a special enrollment period for members of a discontinued plan, December 8 to February 28. Medicare.gov describes a similar period when a plan’s contract is not renewed. I can tell you how it applies to you.
Common questions

Questions from Aetna PPO members

Is my Aetna Medicare PPO really ending?

Aetna says it discontinues some plans each year, and its Medicare Advantage PPO plans in Orange County and Ventura County are among those being discontinued for 2027. Read the notice Aetna sent you to see how it applies to your plan. If you did not get one or you are not sure, call me and we can go through your coverage together.

What happens if I do nothing?

Aetna says that as of January 1, 2027, a member without a new plan is covered only by Original Medicare. Original Medicare does not include most prescription drug coverage, which is why people usually add a separate Part D plan. I can walk you through what that would look like for you.

Do I have to decide by December 7?

Annual Enrollment runs October 15 to December 7, and a change you make in that period starts January 1. Aetna also lists a special enrollment period for members of a discontinued plan, December 8 to February 28. I can go over the dates and how they apply to you.

Can I stay with Aetna?

Aetna may offer other Medicare Advantage plans where you live. They may be HMOs instead of PPOs, which can change which doctors you can see and whether you need referrals. I check what is available at your address.

Will my doctors still be covered?

It depends on the plan you choose. Many PPO members are used to seeing doctors without a medical group or referral. Other plans can work differently, and a doctor can be in or out of a plan separately from the hospital. I check each of your doctors before you enroll.

Can I buy Medigap without health questions?

It may be possible. Medicare.gov says you can have a guaranteed issue right when your Medicare Advantage plan leaves Medicare or stops serving your area, and the window to apply is limited. Whether it applies to you depends on your situation, so I check it with you before you change anything. Keep every letter you receive about the change.

Will switching plans cost me more?

It depends on the plan. Premiums, copays, and your prescriptions all differ. Medigap premiums are usually higher than a Medicare Advantage plan, but you can see any provider that accepts Medicare. I walk through your doctors, your drugs, and the total cost under each option I review so you can decide.

I live in Ventura County. Does this apply to me?

Yes. I help members in both Orange County and Ventura County. Read your Aetna notice, and call me if you want help with it.
