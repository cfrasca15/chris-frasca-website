Common questions

Medicare questions from Aliso Viejo residents

I'm still working at 65. Do I need to sign up for Medicare?

It depends. If your employer has 20 or more employees, you can often delay Part B without a penalty while you are covered by that plan. With fewer than 20, you likely need to enroll right away. I can look at your specific situation.

What about my HSA?

Once you are enrolled in Medicare, you can no longer contribute to a Health Savings Account. Because Part A can be backdated up to six months, people often need to stop contributing earlier than they expect. Worth planning before you apply.

What happens when I retire?

When employment or coverage based on current employment ends, you generally get a Special Enrollment Period to sign up for Part B without a penalty. I help you time it so coverage is continuous.

What does it cost to work with you?

Nothing. Licensed insurance agents are paid a commission by the insurance carrier, not by you. Your premium is the same whether you enroll through an agent or directly with Medicare, and there is no fee for a consultation, plan review, or yearly review.
