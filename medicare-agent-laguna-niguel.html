Common questions

Medicare questions from Laguna Niguel residents

My spouse is younger than I am. Do we enroll at the same time?

Not necessarily. Medicare is based on each person's own 65th birthday. If your spouse is still working and covered by an employer plan, you may be able to coordinate the timing, but the rules depend on the employer and your situation. I walk through it with you.

I'm turning 65 soon. When should I start?

Your Initial Enrollment Period begins three months before the month you turn 65. Starting the conversation a few months earlier gives you room to compare plans without a rush.

Can we do this by video call?

Yes. Phone, video, and in-person meetings are all available, and family members are welcome to join.

What does it cost to work with you?

Nothing. Licensed insurance agents are paid a commission by the insurance carrier, not by you. Your premium is the same whether you enroll through an agent or directly with Medicare, and there is no fee for a consultation, plan comparison, or yearly review.
