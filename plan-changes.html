Common questions

Quick answers

What is AEP, and when does it end?

The Annual Enrollment Period runs October 15 through December 7 every year. During this window, anyone with Medicare can switch Medicare Advantage plans, switch Part D drug plans, or move between Original Medicare and Medicare Advantage — no qualifying event required. Changes take effect January 1. Once December 7 passes, your options narrow significantly.

My Medicare Advantage plan is being discontinued — what happens now?

During Annual Enrollment (October 15 through December 7), you can shop for and enroll in a new plan regardless of the discontinuation timeline your carrier gave you. You don't need a special qualifying event — AEP gives everyone that same flexibility. You can also review your options with me before October 15 so you're ready.

My doctor is no longer in-network — what are my options?

During AEP, you can switch to a plan available in your area that keeps your doctors in-network — no need to qualify for anything special. Worth a quick, free check before you assume you have to change doctors.

What if I miss the December 7 deadline?

After AEP closes, your ability to make changes becomes much more limited — generally only available if you have a specific qualifying event, like a plan discontinuation or a permanent move. That's exactly why it's worth checking your options early, while you have the most flexibility.

Does this cost anything?

No. Licensed insurance agents are paid a commission by the insurance carrier, not by you — your premium is exactly the same whether you enroll through an agent or directly with Medicare.
