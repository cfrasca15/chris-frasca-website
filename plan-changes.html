Common questions

Quick answers

What happens if my Medicare Advantage plan is discontinued?

Medicare requires the carrier to notify you in writing, and you automatically qualify for a Special Enrollment Period to pick a new plan — you won't lose coverage in the gap, but you do need to actively choose a replacement.

My doctor is no longer in-network — what are my options?

Depending on your specific plan and situation, you may qualify for a Special Enrollment Period to switch to a plan that keeps your doctors in-network. Worth a quick, free check before you assume you have to change doctors.

Does this cost anything?

No. Licensed insurance agents are paid a commission by the insurance carrier, not by you — your premium is exactly the same whether you enroll through an agent or directly with Medicare.

How much time do I have to decide?

It depends on which enrollment period applies to your situation — some windows are just a couple of months. The sooner you check, the more options you'll have.
