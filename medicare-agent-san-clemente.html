Common questions

Medicare questions from San Clemente residents

I have TRICARE For Life. Do I still need Medicare?

Yes. TRICARE For Life generally requires that you are enrolled in both Medicare Part A and Part B to keep your coverage. I can help you time enrollment correctly.

I get care through the VA. Can I skip Part B?

VA benefits do not replace Medicare. Skipping Part B can mean a late-enrollment penalty that follows you if you sign up later. It is worth talking through before you decide.

I live near the San Diego County line. Does that matter?

Yes. Medicare Advantage and Part D plans are offered by county, so the county you live in determines which plans are available. I check the plans for your exact address.

What does it cost to work with you?

Nothing. Licensed insurance agents are paid a commission by the insurance carrier, not by you. Your premium is the same whether you enroll through an agent or directly with Medicare, and there is no fee for a consultation, plan comparison, or yearly review.
