Common questions

Medicare questions from Dana Point residents

I split my time between two homes. What should I know?

Original Medicare with a Medigap plan generally works anywhere in the country. Medicare Advantage plans typically expect you to live in the plan's service area and have limited coverage elsewhere. The right choice depends on how much and where you travel.

I just moved to Dana Point. Can I change my plan?

If you permanently move to a new plan service area, you generally qualify for a Special Enrollment Period to choose a plan there. I help you pick one and avoid a gap in coverage.

Do you work with people who recently moved to California?

Yes. Medicare itself is national, but plan options, prices, and networks are local, so it helps to start fresh with your new address.

What does it cost to work with you?

Nothing. Licensed insurance agents are paid a commission by the insurance carrier, not by you. Your premium is the same whether you enroll through an agent or directly with Medicare, and there is no fee for a consultation, plan review, or yearly review.
