Common questions

Medicare questions from Camarillo residents

How do you check my prescriptions?

I enter your medications and doses and look up how each plan covers them, including the tier and cost, and whether your pharmacy is a preferred one. The results can look very different from plan to plan.

Do plans change their drug coverage each year?

Yes. Plans can change their formulary, tiers, and costs every year, and they must tell you in writing each fall. That is why I recommend a review every Annual Enrollment Period.

Is there help with prescription costs?

People with limited income may qualify for Extra Help, a federal program that lowers drug costs. I can tell you whether you might qualify and how to apply.

What does it cost to work with you?

Nothing. Licensed insurance agents are paid a commission by the insurance carrier, not by you. Your premium is the same whether you enroll through an agent or directly with Medicare, and there is no fee for a consultation, plan review, or yearly review.
